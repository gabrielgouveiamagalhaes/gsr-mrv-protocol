**Note on 1.5.** The E2 fraction is not incidental. A provenance audit of the campaign's 107
weighing records found that 34 records covering 458.7 t carry an original retention profile, and
that 73 records covering 349.3 t were reconstructed from a primary source. The physical tickets
for the reconstructed portion are no longer held.

The publisher therefore states the campaign as **808.0 t under official weighing** and does not
assert a per-ticket count. Clause 3 exists because this distinction had to be made about the
publisher's own flagship campaign.
