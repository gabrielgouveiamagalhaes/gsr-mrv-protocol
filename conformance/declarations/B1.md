# Conformance declaration — B1

Filed by the publisher against its second reference campaign. **This declaration fails.** It is
published because a conformance scheme in which the author never fails is not a conformance
scheme.

```
Implementer:  GSR Logística Reversa Naval
Facility:     GSR 999 · Gradim, São Gonçalo / RJ
Campaign:     B1 — Vital Brazil & Itapuca, passenger ferries
Asset type:   Monocoque steel hull
Period:       February – October 2020
Scope:        Whole campaign
Level declared:  [x] NON-CONFORMING   (see stream-scoped declaration below)
```

**Why 1.1 and 1.3 fail.** The campaign reports eight material streams totalling 962.00 t against a
declared LDT of 962.38 t. One stream — 681.765 t of ferrous steel — is weighed per load across 123
expeditions. The other seven are round figures totalling exactly 280 t, which is exactly the
declared LDT less the weighed ferrous mass. That is consistent with those streams having been
sized to close against LDT rather than measured, which is precisely what clause 2.1 prohibits.

The publisher does not hold tickets to correct this and does not intend to reconstruct them.
The record stands as filed, with the failure declared.

## What this declaration is for

Two campaigns, filed honestly, produce one Level 2, one non-conformance, and one stream-scoped
Level 1. That distribution is what an implementer should expect from real historical work, and it
is the reason clause 11.1 exists: without stream scoping, an implementer with one well-measured
stream inside a poorly-measured campaign has no way to report the part that is sound, and the
rational response is to report nothing.
