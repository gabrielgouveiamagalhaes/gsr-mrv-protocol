# Conformance declaration — B1

Filed by the publisher against its second reference campaign. **This declaration fails.** It is
published because a conformance scheme in which the author never fails is not a conformance
scheme.

```
Implementer:  GSR Logística Reversa Naval
Facility:     GSR 999 · Gradim, São Gonçalo / RJ
Campaign:     B1 — Vital Brazil & Itapuca, passenger ferries
Asset type:   Monocoque steel hull
Period:       February – October 2020
Scope:        Whole campaign
Level declared:  [x] NON-CONFORMING   (see stream-scoped declaration below)
```

**Why 1.1 and 1.3 fail.** The campaign reports eight material streams totalling 962.38 t against a
declared LDT of 962.38 t. One stream — 724.695 t of ferrous steel — is weighed per load across 123
expeditions. The other seven total exactly 237.685 t, which is exactly the declared LDT less the
weighed ferrous mass. That is consistent with those streams having been sized to close against LDT
rather than measured, which is precisely what clause 2.1 prohibits.

*Revised in v0.4.* This declaration previously recorded the ferrous stream at 681.765 t and the
seven residual streams at a round 280 t. Both were consequences of the range error described under
clause 7 below. Correcting the ferrous mass moves the residual to 237.685 t and the closure against
LDT becomes exact rather than approximate — so the non-conformance under 2.1 is now more evident,
not less. The ratio moves from `r = 0.708` to `r = 0.753`.

The publisher does not hold tickets to correct this and does not intend to reconstruct them.
The record stands as filed, with the failure declared.

## Clause 7 exercised

| Quantity | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Declared steel mass (source document) | 681,765 kg |
| Sum of line items — authoritative | 724,695 kg |
| Relative difference | **+6.30 %** |
| Against δ = 0.15 | Within tolerance — recorded, not escalated |
| Cause (Requirement 7.2) | Range error: the source spreadsheet's TOTAL cell summed `B1:B118` while load records run to row 125. The seven omitted expeditions (2020-09-30 to 2020-10-08) are exactly 42,930 kg. |

The difference has not been closed by adjusting either figure, in conformance with Requirement 7.1:
the declared figure stays on the record beside the authoritative one.

**This is the case that produced Requirement 7.2.** For three filings this difference sat inside δ
and was therefore never escalated — correctly, under v0.3. It was recorded in the traceability
matrix as a "physical ε of +6.3 %" and no clause obliged anyone to ask why. The cause was exact,
discoverable and arithmetic. A tolerance that excuses investigation converts a bug into a
conformance, and v0.4 closes that: δ decides when a difference becomes a divergence requiring
review, never whether it deserves an explanation.

## What this declaration is for

Two campaigns, filed honestly, produce one Level 2, one non-conformance, and one stream-scoped
Level 1. That distribution is what an implementer should expect from real historical work, and it
is the reason clause 11.1 exists: without stream scoping, an implementer with one well-measured
stream inside a poorly-measured campaign has no way to report the part that is sound, and the
rational response is to report nothing.
